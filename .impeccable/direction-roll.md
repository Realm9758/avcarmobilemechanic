# Direction provenance

**Build:** A V Car Mobile Mechanic, 17 August 2026.
**Outcome:** the standing exit. The house dark-professional canon, accent
Spode cobalt.

## How the decision was taken

The user was offered a structured question round before any code was written,
carrying four options: the plain professional canon (recommended, on the record
of two prior clean choices), and three worlds derived from this business's own
material and cultural ground.

| Option | One-line world | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **House canon, new accent** | Graphite ground, hairline structure, one rationed accent, light-led hero. Divergence by accent only. | **Chosen** |
| The Potteries | Bottle-kiln brick, Spode cobalt transferware, glazed ceramic edges. Deeply local to Stoke. | Not taken. Its colour survived into the canon as the accent. |
| Roadside patrol heritage | Mid-century AA/RAC callout: vitreous enamel badge, cast signage, night tarmac. | Not taken |
| Diagnostic instrument | Scan tool and torque wrench: engineered dark UI, precise numerals, readout green. | Not taken |

That is **three clean choices of canon out of three** for this client
(showroomdetailing 13 Aug, maautoservice 17 Aug, this build 17 Aug). Canon is
now the standing assumption, but the offer stays cheap and keeps being made.

## The roll it superseded

`concept-seed.mjs --scope direction --mode persuade` was run afterwards for the
record, not for the decision: a user-pinned direction beats the roll, always.

- **Seed key:** `4c8951b9`
- **Assigned index:** 3
- **Challengers dealt:** a memory quilt of worn garments; a coiled earthen
  tower; a 1914 wood-type manifesto page; a Dong Ho woodblock print on crushed
  shell ground.

None of them were put to the user, because the user had already chosen. Recorded
here so a future build does not re-deal the same hand and mistake it for new.
